# OPS-AUT-004 Client Authentication Standards - Telephone and Email

> FICTIONAL DOCUMENT. Written for the ARM Copilot prototype. Aurelian Peak Private Bank does not exist, and nothing here is based on any real bank's procedures. These are invented example rules and are not real security guidance.

### 4.1 Principles and authentication levels

Before staff give information or act on an instruction, the client must be authenticated to the level that matches the request. There are four levels: Level 1 for general enquiries, Level 2 for account information, Level 3 for trade, deposit and loan instructions, and Level 4 for high-risk instructions. Each level includes the checks of the levels below it. Caller ID is never enough on its own. If the client's identity is in doubt, stop and escalate to the Desk Head.

### 4.2 Level 1: general enquiries

For questions that do not reveal any account information, such as opening hours or product information, staff confirm the caller's full name and the relationship they have with the bank.

### 4.3 Level 2: account information

To discuss balances, positions or transactions, staff must confirm the Level 1 details and two of the following: the call comes from the registered mobile number, the client's date of birth, the registered email address, or the answer to one security question from the client profile.

### 4.4 Level 3: trade, deposit and loan instructions

To take a trade, deposit or loan instruction, staff must complete Level 2 and also obtain the client's telephone passphrase, which was set up at onboarding. Staff must never read the passphrase aloud, and the passphrase must never be written in an email or a chat message.

### 4.5 Level 4: high-risk instructions

Level 4 applies to the first payment to a new third-party beneficiary, any change to the client's contact details, and any payment above USD 500,000 equivalent. Staff must complete Level 3 and must also call the client back on the registered number held on file. The instruction cannot be released until Operations gives sign-off. A Level 4 instruction is never accepted by email alone.

### 4.6 Email instructions

Instructions by email are accepted only from the registered email address, and only for requests below Level 4. If the amount is USD 100,000 equivalent or more, the instruction must also be confirmed by phone at Level 3 before it is executed.

### 4.7 Failed or incomplete authentication

If the caller fails to answer a question correctly, staff must not say which answer was wrong or give any hint. After 2 failed attempts in the same call, staff must stop, log the incident in the incident register and inform the Desk Head, who decides on next steps. No information is given and no instruction is taken during that call.

### 4.8 Forgotten passphrase

If the client cannot remember the passphrase, staff must not take a trade, deposit or loan instruction on that call. Staff may offer a call-back to the registered number and complete Level 2. The passphrase can only be reset by the Desk Head through recovery verification, and the Client Account desk cannot reset it.
