# OPS-PTR-005 Pre-Trade Procedures and Order-Taking Standards

> FICTIONAL DOCUMENT. Written for the ARM Copilot prototype. Aurelian Peak Private Bank does not exist, and nothing here is based on any real bank's procedures or compliance rules.

### 5.1 Voice-logged call opening script

Every order taken by phone must follow this sequence on the recorded line: (1) tell the client that the call is recorded, (2) authenticate the client at Level 3 under OPS-AUT-004, (3) take the order details required for the product under the order-taking standards in clauses 5.2 to 5.4, and (4) read the full order back to the client and obtain a clear verbal "confirmed" before it is entered.

### 5.2 Order-taking standard: listed equities

For a listed equity order, staff must obtain: the stock code or ISIN, the market, buy or sell, the quantity, whether it is a market order or a limit order, the limit price (for a limit order), the order validity (day order, or good-till-cancelled for up to 30 days), and the settlement account.

### 5.3 Order-taking standard: bonds

For a bond order, staff must obtain: the ISIN, the nominal amount, buy or sell, the price (clean price) or yield, the settlement date, and the settlement account. Staff must also confirm that the nominal amount meets the bond's minimum denomination.

### 5.4 Order-taking standard: FX and time deposits

For an FX order, staff must obtain the currency pair, which currency is bought and which is sold, the amount, the value date, the limit rate (if any) and the settlement instructions. For a time deposit, staff must obtain the currency, the amount, the tenor, the value date and the maturity instruction (roll over principal only, roll over principal and interest, or pay out). Cutoff times for time deposits are in OPS-CUT-001.

### 5.5 Suitability and risk profile validity check

Before taking an order, staff must check that the client's risk profile in the system was reviewed within the last 12 months and that the product's risk rating does not exceed the client's risk profile. If the risk profile is older than 12 months, the order cannot be taken and the RM must refresh the risk profile first. A client cannot waive this check.

### 5.6 Complex products: additional pre-trade steps

For accumulators, decumulators and structured notes, staff must also: confirm in the system that the client has Professional Investor status; email the term sheet and the Product Key Facts statement to the client and obtain the client's acknowledgement before the order is placed; and state the key risks on the recorded line. For an accumulator, the key risks include that the client may have to buy at the strike price even when the market price is lower, that the leverage can multiply losses, and that losses may be larger than the initial investment. For a decumulator, the key risk includes that the client may have to sell at the strike price even when the market price is higher.

### 5.7 Management approval for complex products

An accumulator, decumulator or structured note order needs approval from the RM and the Desk Head before execution. If the notional amount is above USD 2 million equivalent, the Head of Private Banking must also approve. The approval must be recorded in the approval system; email or chat approval is not valid. The order must not be executed until all approvals are recorded. An approval is valid on the day it is given only.

### 5.8 Post-trade confirmation email and account number masking

An order confirmation email must be sent to the client within 30 minutes after execution. In any email, the client account number must be masked so that only the last 4 digits are shown, for example ****1234. The email must not contain the passphrase or any authentication answers.

### 5.9 Order-taking standard: investment funds

For an investment fund order, staff must obtain: the fund name or ISIN and the share class, whether it is a subscription (buy) or a redemption (sell), the amount in cash or the number of units (for a redemption, a number of units or all units), the dealing currency and the settlement account. Staff must tell the client that the price is not known until after the fund's dealing cutoff, and that an order received after the fund's dealing cutoff is dealt on the next dealing day. Staff must confirm that the client has received the fund's Key Facts Statement. The risk profile check in clause 5.5 applies to every fund order.

### 5.10 Stop-loss orders

A stop-loss order is accepted only for Hong Kong listed equities. In addition to the details in clause 5.2, staff must obtain the stop trigger price and whether the order becomes a market order or a limit order once the trigger price is reached. A stop-loss order may be valid for up to 30 days. Staff must tell the client that a stop-loss order does not guarantee execution at the trigger price.

### 5.11 Product suitability form

The product suitability form must be completed and saved before the order is entered. It records: the client name and client reference number; the date of the client's last risk profile review, which must be within the last 12 months (clause 5.5); the client's risk profile category; the product name, ISIN and risk rating; the order amount as a percentage of the client's total relationship assets; the reason for the trade in the client's own words; and whether the product's risk rating exceeds the client's risk profile (yes or no). If the answer is yes, the order cannot proceed. If the order amount is more than 20% of the client's total relationship assets in a single product, add a concentration note and obtain the Desk Head's approval. The RM must review and sign the form in the approval system on the same day.

### 5.12 Structured products: documents and call checklist

Before the order, the client must receive by email the term sheet, the Product Key Facts statement and the product risk disclosure statement, and must acknowledge them (clause 5.6). Staff must complete the product suitability form (clause 5.11) and check that the client has Professional Investor status. On the recorded call, staff must: say that the call is recorded and authenticate the client at Level 3 (clause 5.1); state the key risks (clause 5.6); confirm that the client has read the documents; and read the full order back and obtain a clear verbal "confirmed". After the trade, the confirmation email must be sent within 30 minutes (clause 5.8), Operations issues the contract note by the next business day, and the term sheet, the acknowledgement and the suitability form are filed with the order record.

### 5.13 Preparing for a client call

Before calling a client, or before taking a call, review the client profile in the CRM. The background summary shows the source of assets, business or corporate activity, family situation and notable past transactions. The investor profile shows investment knowledge and experience, investment objectives, and the risk profile with its review date. Use this information only to serve the client. Do not discuss one client's information with another client, and do not keep notes about clients outside the CRM.
