# OPS-PRC-002 Client Tiers and Pricing Schedule

> FICTIONAL DOCUMENT. Written for the ARM Copilot prototype. Aurelian Peak Private Bank does not exist, and nothing here is based on any real bank's procedures, rates or fees.

### 2.1 Client tier definitions

Every client is assigned one pricing tier based on total relationship assets with the bank, measured as the average month-end assets over the last 3 months:

- Tier A: USD 20 million equivalent or more.
- Tier B: USD 5 million equivalent or more, but less than USD 20 million.
- Tier C: USD 1 million equivalent or more, but less than USD 5 million.

The minimum to be a private banking client is USD 1 million equivalent. The tier is shown on the client profile in the CRM.

### 2.2 Tier review and changes

Tiers are reviewed on 1 January and 1 July each year. An upgrade takes effect from the first business day after the review is confirmed by the Desk Head. A downgrade has a 3-month grace period, during which the client keeps the previous tier's pricing. A tier change outside the review dates needs the written approval of the Head of Private Banking.

### 2.3 Negotiated pricing overrides the schedule

If a client has a signed Client Pricing Agreement (CPA) on file, the prices in the CPA override this schedule for the products it covers. Always check whether a CPA exists on the client profile before quoting any price. For products that the CPA does not cover, this schedule applies.

### 2.4 FX spreads

Spreads on spot FX for amounts up to USD 5 million equivalent:

| Tier | Major pairs | Other pairs |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| A | 5 bps | 10 bps |
| B | 8 bps | 13 bps |
| C | 12 bps | 17 bps |

Major pairs are USD/HKD, EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD and USD/CNH. All other pairs are "other pairs". For amounts above USD 5 million equivalent, the dealing desk gives a manual quote and the schedule does not apply.

### 2.5 Equity and bond brokerage

Brokerage rates by tier:

| Product | Tier A | Tier B | Tier C |
|---------|--------|--------|--------|
| Hong Kong listed equities | 0.15% (min HKD 150) | 0.20% (min HKD 200) | 0.25% (min HKD 250) |
| US listed equities | 0.10% (min USD 20) | 0.15% (min USD 25) | 0.20% (min USD 30) |
| Bonds, secondary market | 0.10% of nominal (min USD 100) | 0.15% of nominal (min USD 100) | 0.20% of nominal (min USD 100) |

Time deposit placements carry no fee.

### 2.6 Custody fees

Custody fees are charged quarterly on the securities held in custody, at these annual rates: Tier A 0.10%, Tier B 0.15% and Tier C 0.20%.

### 2.7 Structured products must not be quoted verbally

For accumulators, decumulators and other structured products, no separate brokerage is charged. The dealing margin is built into the terms and disclosed in the term sheet. The margin does not change with the client tier. Staff must not quote or estimate the margin verbally on a call. They must refer the client to the term sheet.

### 2.8 Discount approval limits

Staff may not offer a discount to the schedule on their own. Discounts off the schedule need approval as follows: up to 20% needs the Relationship Manager (RM), more than 20% and up to 40% needs the Desk Head, and more than 40% needs the Head of Private Banking. An approved discount must be recorded in the CRM before it is quoted to the client, and it applies to that trade only unless it is added to a CPA.
